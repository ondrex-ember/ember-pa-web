Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Ember Pheno app (the "Application") for Android, published by Ondřej Panuška, Ember PA (the "Service Provider", www.ember-pa.cz). The Application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

🔒 Our commitment: Ember Pheno has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not connect to the internet by itself. Your plants, records, notes and photos are stored only on your device and the Service Provider never receives them.
Information Stored on Your Device

To work as a growing diary, the Application stores the following data locally, in its private storage on your device:

  • Plants: names, categories, varieties, location labels you type, start dates, growth stages and your notes
  • Records of care and observations (for example watering, feeding, measurements, problems, harvests and ratings) with dates and notes
  • Photos you choose to attach, stored in reduced size
  • Items you create: plant groups, custom categories, custom tasks, rating criteria, rules and intervals, and your storage (stock) records
  • App preferences: language, and whether the daily notification is on and at what time

All of this stays on your device. The Application does not transmit it to the Service Provider or to any third party.

What Is Not Collected
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by the Application
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other sensitive device data
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone
📍 Location: The Application does not request or use your location. A location label on a plant is just text that you type yourself (for example "balcony").
🤖 AI: Task schedules, statistics and scores are calculated on your device from your own entries using fixed rules. No data is sent to any AI service.
Internet Access

The Application does not request the Internet permission and does not connect to the internet by itself.

The About screen contains links to websites of the Service Provider and its other projects. A link opens in your web browser, outside the Application. Your browser and the visited website then handle that visit under their own privacy policies. The Application sends nothing with it.

Permissions

The Application may ask for the following permission, only for the purpose described:

  • Notifications (Android 13+): a local daily summary of your tasks. It is scheduled on your device; there is no push server. Like any notification, it may be visible on your lock screen depending on your Android settings

The Application does not request the camera or storage permissions. Instead:

  • Photos: when you add a photo, the Application opens your device's camera app or the system photo picker and receives only the picture you take or choose. The picture is stored in the Application's private storage. A temporary copy used while taking a picture is kept in the Application's cache
  • Backup files: saving and restoring a backup uses the Android file picker. The Application can access only the file you create or select

Android may also grant the Application standard permissions needed for scheduled background work (for example, to restore the reminder schedule after the device restarts). These do not give access to your personal data.

Backup and Export

In Settings → Backup you can save a ZIP backup (including photos) and restore from it. A backup is created only when you start it, in a location you choose, and the Application has no access to that file afterwards. The backup is an ordinary ZIP file and is not password protected. It contains your notes and photos, so store it as carefully as any personal document.

Android Automatic Backup

If automatic backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may copy the Application's database (your plants, records, notes and settings stored in it) to your Google account or to a new device when you switch phones. Photos and the language preference are not included.

This is done by Android and Google under your device's backup settings and Google's Privacy Policy. The Service Provider does not receive or access these copies. You can turn this off in your Android backup settings.

Third Party Services

The Application is distributed through Google Play. Google Play Services may collect certain technical and device information independently, according to Google's own Privacy Policy. The Application has no control over this data collection.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

Beyond Google Play Services, the Application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not work with data brokers.

Opt-Out and Data Deletion

Everything is under your control on your device:

  • Settings → Delete all data erases your plants, records, photos and settings from the device
  • Uninstalling the Application removes all data stored by it, including photos
  • Backup files you saved stay where you saved them; delete them yourself
  • Copies kept by Android or Google backup are managed in your Google account and device backup settings
  • The daily notification can be switched off in the Application or in Android settings at any time
Data Retention

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the Application. The Service Provider holds no copy of it.

If you have any concerns, contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Your Rights

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase on its side; you manage all data directly in the Application. If you believe your rights under data protection law (such as the GDPR) have been affected, you can contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz or lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.cz).

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Security

Data is stored in the Application's private storage on your device, protected by the Android app sandbox. The Application has no PIN or app lock of its own, so protect your device with a screen lock. If you use backups, remember that a backup file is not password protected, and that Android's automatic backup is subject to Google's own security and privacy practices.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time and the changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. If features that process data outside your device (for example advertising, accounts or cloud sync) are ever introduced, this policy and the Data safety section in Google Play will be updated before their release. Continued use of the Application is deemed approval of all changes.
