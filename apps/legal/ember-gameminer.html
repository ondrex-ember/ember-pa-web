Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Ember Game Miner app (the "Application") for Android, published by Ondřej Panuška, Ember PA (the "Service Provider", www.ember-pa.cz). The Application helps families motivate children: children earn credits by completing real-world tasks and spend them on rewards agreed with their parents. The Application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

🔒 Our commitment: Ember Game Miner has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your tasks, rewards, credits, history and settings are stored only on your device and the Service Provider never receives them.
Information Stored on Your Device

To work, the Application stores the following data locally, in its private storage on your device:

  • Tasks (activities that earn credits) and rewards (items that spend credits): names, descriptions, icons, rates, daily limits, costs, maximum times and the alarm sound you chose
  • Credit balance and the history of completed tasks and redeemed rewards
  • The state of running timers (their start times), so that a timer keeps counting when the Application is closed
  • App preferences: theme, language and display settings

The Application does not ask for any personal information such as a name, e-mail address or date of birth. Texts you type yourself (for example the names of tasks and rewards) are stored exactly as you entered them. All of this stays on your device. The Application does not transmit it to the Service Provider or to any third party.

What Is Not Collected
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by the Application
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other sensitive device data
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone
🌐 Internet: The Application does not need an internet connection to work and does not connect to the Service Provider's or any third-party server.
📍 Location: The Application does not request or use your location.
🤖 AI: The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence technologies to process your data or provide features.
Permissions

The Application may ask for the following permissions, each only for the purpose described:

  • Notifications (Android 13+): to show a notification when a countdown or timer ends. Notifications are created locally on your device; there is no push server
  • Alarms and reminders (exact alarms): so that a countdown can ring at the exact moment it ends, even when the device is idle or the Application is closed. The alarm is scheduled locally by Android, and no data leaves your device. If you revoke this permission in Android settings, timers may end later or without an alarm
  • Sounds: the Application plays an alarm sound from the sounds available on your device. Only a reference to the sound you selected is stored; the Application does not read or copy your audio files
Children and Families

The Application is designed for families. Children use it together with a parent or guardian, who sets up and manages the tasks and rewards and is responsible for the content entered.

The Application does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no social or sharing features, and no data about children leaves the device through the Application.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Third Party Services

The Application is distributed through Google Play. Google Play Services may collect certain technical and device information independently, according to Google's own Privacy Policy. The Application has no control over this data collection.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

Beyond Google Play Services, the Application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not work with data brokers.

Device Backup

If the system backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the Application's data in your device backup stored in your Google account. This is handled by Android and Google under your device settings and Google's Privacy Policy. The Service Provider has no access to such backups. You can turn the system backup off in Android settings.

Opt-Out and Data Deletion

Everything is under your control on your device:

  • Resetting the Application in its settings erases your tasks, rewards, credits and history
  • Clearing the Application's data in Android settings, or uninstalling the Application, removes all data stored by it
  • Notifications and alarms can be switched off at any time in Android settings
Data Retention

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it, reset the Application or uninstall it. The Service Provider holds no copy of it.

If you have any concerns, contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Your Rights

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase on its side; you manage all data directly in the Application. If you believe your rights under data protection law (such as the GDPR) have been affected, you can contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz or lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.cz).

Security

Data is stored in the Application's private storage on your device. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep it away from unsupervised use if you do not want others to change tasks and rewards. Because the Service Provider holds no copy of your data, it cannot recover data from a lost, damaged or reset device.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time and the changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. If features that process data outside your device (for example advertising, accounts or cloud sync) are ever introduced, or if new features change what the Application stores on your device, this policy and the Data safety section in Google Play will be updated before their release. Continued use of the Application is deemed approval of all changes.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you acknowledge that Ember Game Miner stores your data only on your device, does not collect or transmit personal data to the Service Provider, and that Google Play Services may independently collect certain technical information as described in Google's own Privacy Policy.
