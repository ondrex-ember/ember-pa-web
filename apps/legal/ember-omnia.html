Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Ember Omnia – Evacuation Bag app (the "Application") for Android, published by Ondřej Panuška, Ember PA (the "Service Provider", www.ember-pa.cz). The Application is provided as a free service and is intended for use "AS IS".

🔒 Our commitment: Ember Omnia has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Your household data, items, notes and photos are stored only on your device and the Service Provider never receives them.
Information Stored on Your Device

To work as an evacuation-bag tracker, the Application stores the following data locally, in its private storage on your device:

  • Household profile: number of adults, children, seniors and pets (by type) and the chosen number of days of self-sufficiency
  • Categories and items: names, quantities, expiry dates, check intervals, notes and packed status
  • Photos you choose to attach to items
  • App preferences: theme, language, font size and notification lead time
  • An optional PIN, stored in encrypted form and used only to confirm sensitive actions inside the Application
  • Cached news and World Health data, so the Info tab can show the last known state offline

All of this stays on your device. The Application does not transmit it to the Service Provider or to any third party.

What Is Not Collected
  • No user accounts or registration
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by the Application
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, microphone or other sensitive device data
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone
📍 Location: The Application does not request or use your location. The Info tab is not personalised by location.
🤖 AI: The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence technologies to process your data or provide features.
Internet Access (Info Tab)

Only the Info tab connects to the internet, to download publicly available information:

  • News: a public RSS news feed (currently BBC World News)
  • World Health: public data from GDACS (EU Joint Research Centre / UN), USGS (earthquakes), the World Health Organization (disease outbreak news), Open-Meteo (air quality), UNHCR (refugee statistics) and the World Bank (poverty statistics). ReliefWeb (UN OCHA) may be used as an additional source

These are ordinary downloads. The Application sends no household data, items, notes, photos, PIN or personal identifiers with them. As with any internet request, the provider of each source can technically see your IP address and standard request data and handles it under its own privacy policy. The air-quality request uses fixed reference coordinates (Prague), never your location. Articles you open from the news feed open in your browser, outside the Application. The list of sources may change as the Application evolves.

Permissions

The Application may ask for the following permissions, each only for the purpose described:

  • Notifications (Android 13+): local reminders about expiring items, recurring checks and the annual review. They are scheduled on your device; there is no push server
  • Camera or photo picker: only when you add a photo to an item. The photo stays on your device
  • Internet: only for the Info tab, as described above
Backup and Export

You can export a ZIP backup (including photos) and restore from it. An export happens only when you start it, to a location you choose, and the Application has no access to that file afterwards. The backup file contains your notes and photos, so store it as carefully as any personal document.

Third Party Services

The Application is distributed through Google Play. Google Play Services may collect certain technical and device information independently, according to Google's own Privacy Policy. The Application has no control over this data collection.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

Beyond Google Play Services and the public information sources listed above, the Application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not work with data brokers.

Opt-Out and Data Deletion

Everything is under your control on your device:

  • Settings → Reset app erases your profile, categories, items and PIN
  • Uninstalling the Application removes all data stored by it, including photos
  • Backup files you exported stay where you saved them; delete them yourself
  • Notifications can be switched off at any time in Android settings
Data Retention

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it, reset the Application or uninstall it. The Service Provider holds no copy of it.

If you have any concerns, contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Your Rights

Because the Service Provider does not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase on its side; you manage all data directly in the Application. If you believe your rights under data protection law (such as the GDPR) have been affected, you can contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz or lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (www.uoou.cz).

Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us at ember@ember-pa.cz.

Security

Data is stored in the Application's private storage on your device and the optional PIN is stored in encrypted form. The Application is excluded from Android's automatic backup (Google backup and device-to-device transfer), so its data is not copied to your Google account by the system. Protect your device with a screen lock. A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered by the Service Provider; you can clear the Application's data in Android settings or reinstall it.
